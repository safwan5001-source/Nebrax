## 0. Conventions

* **Language precedence.** Every ZATCA English PDF used here carries the disclaimer that it is an unofficial translation and that the Arabic version prevails ([RES-EN] every page; [TIG-EN], [RSG-EN], [IEG-EN] cover pages). Where I checked the Arabic original the row says so; where only the English translation was read, the row cites the English page and the Arabic file is listed in the inventory for the consolidator.
* **Verification labels.** `VERIFIED-FROM-SOURCE` = I read the statement in the downloaded official document/page at the cited location. `VERIFIED (quoted in ZATCA guideline)` = the underlying instrument (e.g. the GCC Unified VAT Agreement) was not obtained, but the statement is quoted/paraphrased in an official ZATCA document that I did read. `RECALLED-UNVERIFIED` = from memory or from a non-official/secondary snippet; not read in an official source.
* **Short keys** in the `Source` / `URL` columns point to the inventory in §1 (the inventory row carries the full URL, version, sha256). Page numbers are the page labels printed inside the document (not PDF reader indices).
* **Applies-to tags:** VAT-ENGINE (rates, tax points, amounts, returns), ACCOUNTING (ledger/records/retention), PHASE1 (generation requirements), PHASE2 (integration/clearance/reporting), SECURITY (tamper-resistance, keys, access), OPS (onboarding, notifications, contingency, filing operations), UX (what the user must see / be able to enter).
* Nothing in this register is legal advice; it is a cited map of what the official texts say, for the other audit streams to test the ERP against.

**What applies to a new SaaS ERP vendor's tenants today (10 Oct 2026):** every tenant is under Phase 1 obligations from the day it is VAT-registered (R1-010/011). Any tenant whose VAT-taxable revenue exceeded SAR 187,500 in any of 2022-2025 is in Wave 25 or an earlier wave; Waves 1-24 integration deadlines have all passed (latest 30 Jun 2026), and Wave 25 must be integrated by 1 Feb 2027. Because SAR 187,500 equals the voluntary-registration threshold ([TIG] 3.3 p.11), essentially every established registered business is now in scope; only businesses whose first revenue year is 2026 are not yet in a wave and remain Phase-1-only until notified (R1-037, [DG] FAQ p.58 — Phase 2 "not required ... until notified", voluntary early adoption recommended). ZATCA's statutory promise is ≥6 months' notice per wave (R1-055). A new ERP therefore has to ship both Phase 1 and full Phase 2 (clearance + reporting) for all tenants.

## 5. Unresolved items and conflicts between sources

**Could not obtain (see inventory last row):** BOE legal-portal record of the VAT Law (503); ZATCA bilingual IR and English VAT Manual (dead links); Data Dictionary workbook; Arabic FAQ; Unified GCC VAT Agreement; Royal Order on State-borne VAT for citizens; official pages for waves 2-5, 7-12, 14-19 (page IDs resolve to soft-404 "page not found" shells); any Wave 26 announcement (none found). The Official Gazette publication date of Board Res. 24-06-01 (reported by advisers as 18 Apr 2025, with one provision deferred to 1 Jan 2026) is RECALLED-UNVERIFIED — the consolidated IR PDF (April 2025) does not state it.

**Conflicts / discrepancies noted:**
1. *Simplified-invoice timing.* [IR] Art. 53(7)(b) requires issuance at the date of supply or receipt, whichever earlier; [IEG-EN] 11.1 p.61 says simplified invoices may be issued "by no later than 15 days following the end of the month" — the IR (Arabic, binding) prevails; the IEG wording is loose.
2. *Credit-note adjustment timing.* [IR] Art. 40(5): decrease in the period of the event or the credit-note period, whichever **later**; [RSG-EN] 6.1 p.32 says "whichever is earlier". IR prevails.
3. *Incorrect-return penalty.* [VATL] Art. 42(1) and [WEB-PEN]: 50% of the difference; [TIG-EN] 11 p.50: 25% base, variable 0-50% under Board rules (consistent with Art. 42(2) discretion). Report the statutory 50% with the Board scale as the applied practice.
4. *Records fine.* [VATL] Art. 45(1) says the fine is "for each tax period"; [WEB-PEN] and [TIG] omit this.
5. *TIG rounding example.* [TIG-EN] 4.2.4 Example 6 (p.21) contains arithmetically inconsistent figures (1,091.30 → "1,195.24"); use the rule text and the [RSG] example, not that example.
6. *Resolution dating.* [RES-EN]/[RES-AR] bodies cite Governor Decision 62738 (23/11/1443H = 22 Jun 2022); the file name and ZATCA page say 19 May 2023; the XML/Security standards are expressly "to the E-Invoicing resolution dated 2023-05-19". Treat 19 May 2023 as the in-force version.
7. *[REG-EN]* splits Art. 4(A) items (3)/(4) incorrectly and uses "Governor of the General Authority of Zakat and Tax" (pre-merger name); AR text is authoritative.
8. *Buyer VAT number on tax invoices* — [RES-EN] Annex 2 5.3 says "Not mandatory for internal supplies" with no definition; [XML] BR-KSA-44 only validates format when present. Treat as conditional (registered buyers must have it; [FAQ-21] p.5).
9. *Rounding at legal level* — the IR contains no rounding article; the "nearest halala" rule is guideline-level ([TIG] 4.2.4, [RSG] 4.1) and the half-up/2-decimal rule is technical ([XML] ch.10). No conflict, but the ERP cannot cite the IR for rounding.
10. *VATEX gaps* — [XML] v1.2 has no exemption-reason code for IR Art. 32 bis (special zones/suspension), Art. 36 bis(2) (diplomatic missions), or the Art. 33(3) tourist-refund services; how ZATCA expects these to be encoded is NOT VERIFIED (R2 to check Data Dictionary/SDK).
11. *FAQ age* — [FAQ-21] (June 2021) still lists "integration not applicable" for Phase 1 and pre-dates waves; use only for Phase-1 statements.
12. *Language of human-readable dates* — [TIG] 4.2.1 allows day/month/year numerals; e-invoice XML and Annex 2 require YYYY-MM-DD. Both are correct in their layer (print vs data).

## 6. Top 25 requirements most likely to be violated by a typical new ERP

1. R1-028 / R1-217 — allowing edit/delete/"cancel" of an issued invoice instead of credit-note + re-issue.
2. R1-114 — no control that credit/debit notes are issued by the 15th of the month after the event (new 2024/25 rule).
3. R1-079 — simplified (B2C/POS) invoices generated later than the supply/receipt date (e.g. end-of-day batch).
4. R1-040 / R1-214 — simplified documents not reported within 24 h (no durable queue/retry, no evidence log).
5. R1-039 / R1-042 — sharing B2B invoices (PDF/e-mail) before clearance, or sharing a non-PDF/A-3 rendering.
6. R1-029 / R1-030 / R1-044 — ICV/PIH chain broken across branches, devices, drafts, voided documents or rejected submissions.
7. R1-056 / R1-057 — resubmitting a rejected XML with the same ICV/hash, or submitting the same document twice.
8. R1-064 — "draft/approval" workflows that generate the ZATCA document before finalisation or back-date the issue date.
9. R1-099 / R1-100 — issue time missing / wrong format; dates not YYYY-MM-DD in XML.
10. R1-137 — VAT total not in SAR on foreign-currency invoices, or conversion not at the SAMA daily rate on the tax-due date.
11. R1-138 / R1-139 / R1-212 — rounding per line vs per invoice inconsistencies; half-up not used; penalty row #6 for consumer invoices.
12. R1-106 — VAT computed before discounts, or invoice-level discounts not reflected in taxable amount per rate.
13. R1-083 (h)/(k) / R1-098 — taxable amount per rate/exemption missing; no "special tax treatment" narration for zero-rated/exempt lines.
14. R1-158 / R1-095 — VAT category codes/VATEX reason codes absent or wrong (Z without reason; O not at 0%).
15. R1-092 / R1-226 — buyer additional ID and national-address elements missing for unregistered B2B buyers (persistent warnings → audit).
16. R1-224 / R1-227 / R1-225 — VAT-number format (15 digits, 3…3), 4-digit building and 5-digit postal code, seller CRN not validated in master data.
17. R1-090 / R1-066 — invoices issued in branch name instead of registered legal name; group VAT number not used by group members.
18. R1-094 / R1-232 — citizen education/health invoices without name + National ID and VATEX-SA-EDU/HEA.
19. R1-065 — advance-payment invoices (386) and later adjustment (PrepaidAmount, KSA-31..34) not supported; VAT on advances not accounted at receipt (R1-132).
20. R1-120 / R1-184 — credit notes after the return booked in the wrong period/box (Amount vs Adjustments).
21. R1-122 / R1-121 — no input-tax reversal after 12 months unpaid; bad-debt relief without the IR conditions.
22. R1-165 / R1-166 — blocked-expense categories and proportional deduction not modelled; box 7 overstated.
23. R1-191 / R1-192 — records/e-invoices hosted outside KSA without KSA access terminal; no tamper-evident audit trail; data not extractable on request.
24. R1-047 / R1-053 / R1-213 — not all devices/branches onboarded as units; CSID renewal/revocation not managed; key material exportable (R1-048).
25. R1-052 / R1-061 / R1-063 / R1-219 — no incident-notification workflow (ZATCA form) and no documented contingency (uncleared invoice handling, manual invoice re-generation).

*Honourable mentions:* R1-017/R1-203 (tenants without VAT number printing "tax invoice"), R1-104 (certificate display on e-commerce store), R1-034/R1-082 (non-Arabic invoices), R1-174/R1-175 (monthly vs quarterly period, deadline), R1-177 (SAR 15,000 correction threshold and 20-day notification).
