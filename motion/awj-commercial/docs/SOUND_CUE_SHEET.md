# AWJ «معاملة واحدة» — Sound-design cue sheet

Picture is locked to these frames (60 fps). All cues are to be **originally designed or
licensed**; no copyrighted music. The POS feedback sounds in `web/public/sounds/pos/` can be
used as source material for the UI ticks (they are the product's own sounds).

## Music direction

- **Tempo:** 120 BPM (1 beat = 30 frames) — every major cut in the picture lands on a beat
  or half-beat: 90 · 180 · 240 · 330 · 390 · 480 · 570 · 600 · 720 · 780.
- **Character:** restrained, percussive, precise. Sub-bass pulses, muted plucks, clock-like
  hi-hat ticks, dry felt piano or mallet. No risers-for-the-sake-of-risers, no EDM drops,
  no "AI whoosh" beds.
- **Shape:** dense and syncopated (0–4 s) → locked groove (4–9.5 s) → open, wider pads
  (9.5–12.3 s) → everything drops out, one low hit + single sustained note (12.3–15 s).
- **Mix:** leave 1–4 kHz clear for VO/supers if a voice-over is added later.
