## 1. Stripe — financial operations UX

Stripe's Dashboard and Invoicing docs set out a strict document lifecycle: you can edit a draft, but a finalized invoice is immutable and you correct it with a credit note or a void. Amounts travel as integers in minor units. The Stripe Apps design system documents how to use lists and tables, empty and loading states, action placement and feedback (toast or banner).

| # | Rule / pattern | Documented detail | Source URL | Implication for AWJ (inference) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Five-state invoice lifecycle with status-gated actions | Statuses are draft, open, paid, void and uncollectible. "The actions you can take on an invoice depend on its status." Each status lists the actions it allows. | https://docs.stripe.com/invoicing/overview | Drive every document's action bar from a status→allowed-actions table (server-provided) and never infer actions in the UI. This matches AWJ's existing `*_action` contract pattern. |
| 2 | Edit only in draft; finalization freezes | You can only edit an invoice in draft. Finalizing sets it to open, ensures a number exists and makes certain properties immutable. | https://docs.stripe.com/invoicing/integration/workflow-transitions | The invoice editor should be a draft-only surface. Posting should look like a clear one-way step that confirms the number, the freeze, and the ledger and ZATCA effects. |
| 3 | Snapshot of customer data at finalization | Finalizing copies the customer's address, email, name, phone, shipping, tax-exempt status and tax IDs onto the invoice and makes them immutable. | https://docs.stripe.com/invoicing/integration/workflow-transitions | This mirrors AWJ's `party_snapshot`. The detail page should show snapshot data and flag when the live partner data differs. |
| 4 | Corrections go through credit notes, not edits | To change the amount after finalization, use credit notes. They work on open or paid invoices and never void or replace the original. | https://docs.stripe.com/invoicing/dashboard/credit-notes | Show "Issue credit note" as the main correction path on posted invoices. Present any credit note in place of the original invoice's actions, not as an edit. |
| 5 | Void keeps the paper trail | A voided invoice "maintains a paper trail" and you can still find it by number. Reports count it as zero-value. Only open or uncollectible invoices can be voided. | https://docs.stripe.com/invoicing/overview | AWJ cancellation and reversal states should stay searchable and visible in lists, with a distinct badge, and must never disappear. |
| 6 | Line-linked credits preferred | Stripe recommends crediting invoice line items rather than a custom line, because a custom line "can make reporting and tracking difficult." The sum of credit notes cannot exceed the invoice total. | https://docs.stripe.com/invoicing/dashboard/credit-notes | The credit-note editor should start from the source lines with remaining-creditable amounts, and show the cap before submission. |
| 7 | Badge vs canonical status can differ | Open invoices "can display a different badge, such as Past due or Retrying". A list can show Pending where the detail page shows Open. | https://docs.stripe.com/invoicing/overview | Separate the stored status from a derived display badge, such as "Overdue", and expose a tooltip that explains the derived badge. |
| 8 | Draft autosave on exit | "Whenever you exit the invoice editor, Stripe saves a draft." A "Review invoice" step comes before sending. | https://docs.stripe.com/invoicing/dashboard | Editors should never lose work: autosave drafts, and show an explicit review step before posting. |
| 9 | Minor units in the API; currency-specific exponents | The API expects amounts "in the currency's minor unit", for example `1000` = 10 USD. Zero-decimal currencies such as JPY are not multiplied. ISK and UGX are kept as legacy two-decimal values. | https://docs.stripe.com/currencies | This validates AWJ's halala bigint rule. Display formatting must take its exponent from the currency, not from a hard-coded 2. |
| 10 | Search with typed filters and operators | Dashboard search supports `amount:`, `number:`, `status:`, `date:` and similar prefixes, plus `>`, `<`, `..` ranges and `is:invoice`. Dates accept natural phrases such as "last week". | https://docs.stripe.com/dashboard/search | Give the global search field-prefixed queries (number, amount range, status) and natural-language dates. Accept Arabic-Indic digits by normalizing them. |
| 11 | Keyboard shortcut index | Pressing `?` lists the available keyboard shortcuts for common actions. | https://docs.stripe.com/dashboard/basics | Ship a `?` shortcut sheet. Shortcuts must be discoverable, not tribal knowledge. |
| 12 | List → detail; DataTable for multi-attribute lists | Use a compact List in narrow drawers and a DataTable "for structured data with multiple columns, pagination, or batch selection". A row click navigates to the detail view. | https://docs.stripe.com/stripe-apps/patterns/lists | Use the table as the default index and side panels for compact related lists. Row click should open detail, with peek (see Linear) as an option. |
| 13 | Action placement | Put actions in the header so they stay visible while content scrolls. A detail page puts the primary action first. Section-specific actions belong in that section's module. | https://docs.stripe.com/stripe-apps/patterns/action-buttons | Use a sticky page header for document actions, keep line-section actions local, and allow one primary action. |
| 14 | Empty state copy rules | Explain why the view is empty and offer the next step. The title says what is missing, with a period. Descriptions stay under 14 words. The action matches the title. Use different messages for "no data yet" and "no results match your filters". | https://docs.stripe.com/stripe-apps/patterns/empty-state | Apply directly to AWJ lists, in Arabic and English. Treat "filtered empty" and "first-use empty" as separate states. |
| 15 | Loading without flash; pending buttons | Show feedback immediately. Scope the spinner to its region. Delay the spinner 200–300 ms to avoid flashing. Keep tab bars visible. Button `pending` disables the button "to prevent double submission". | https://docs.stripe.com/stripe-apps/patterns/loading | Posting and payment buttons must disable while pending. Use section-level skeletons, and never blank out a whole page. |
| 16 | Toast vs banner; errors ≠ empty | Toasts are temporary, triggered by an action, and ≤30 characters. Banners persist and say what action is needed. Distinguish errors from empty states, and never let loading run without a timeout. | https://docs.stripe.com/stripe-apps/patterns/communicating-state ; https://docs.stripe.com/stripe-apps/review-requirements | Use toasts to confirm success and banners for blockers such as a locked period or a closed gate. A network failure must never render as "No invoices". |

Custom styling is intentionally limited "to ensure a high accessibility bar", including restricted colours for contrast (https://docs.stripe.com/stripe-apps/design). This supports AWJ's single-accent token discipline.

## 2. Linear — keyboard-first, views and triage

Linear's docs describe a keyboard-first workspace. A `Cmd/Ctrl K` command menu acts on the current selection, filters and display options are separate, filtered views can be saved and shared, `Space` opens a peek preview, and triage and inbox queues are built for rapid single-key decisions. The Linear Method states product principles such as "Purpose-built" and "Simple first, then powerful".

| # | Rule / pattern | Documented detail | Source URL | Implication for AWJ (inference) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Command menu acts on selection | Select one or more issues, then press `Cmd/Ctrl K` "to open the command bar and select the preferred action", or right-click for the same actions. | https://linear.app/docs/select-issues | AWJ's ⌘K should do more than navigate. It should run actions on the highlighted row or selected rows, such as post, print, export or assign, filtered by permission. |
| 2 | Scoped prefixes in the command menu | Typing `i` and a space focuses the menu on issues. `p`, `u`, `t`, `l`, `f` and `d` do the same for projects, users, teams, labels, favorites and documents. | https://linear.app/docs/search | Add entity prefixes for invoices, partners, products and accounts. Arabic users need a non-Latin-dependent alternative, such as a scope chip. |
| 3 | Highlight ≠ select; keyboard range selection | Move the highlight with ↑/↓ or J/K, select with `X`, extend with Shift+arrows, select all with `Cmd/Ctrl A`, and clear with `Esc`. | https://linear.app/docs/select-issues | Dense AWJ tables need a keyboard row cursor, with arrow keys mirrored correctly in RTL, and separate from checkbox selection. |
| 4 | Filter first, then bulk | Refine with filters, then select all and run an action. Common bulk actions "show up at the bottom" in a bulk toolbar. | https://linear.app/docs/select-issues | Use a bottom bulk-action bar for multi-select. Bulk posting or approval must still pass through server preview and confirmation. |
| 5 | Filters encoded in the URL | Applied filters are reflected in the browser URL, so a copied link reopens the same filtered view. View options and quick filters are excluded from the URL. | https://linear.app/docs/filters | Encode list filters in query params so accountants can share "unpaid invoices for March" as a link. |
| 6 | Filters vs display options are distinct | Display options control grouping, ordering, layout and visible properties. "Filters will refine the list"; display options "hide or show data". | https://linear.app/docs/display-options | Separate "which rows" (filters) from "how rows look" (columns, density, grouping) in the toolbar UI. |
| 7 | Personal vs workspace default display | Changes made without "Set as default" stay personal. "Set as default" applies to every member, and "Reset to default" reverts. | https://linear.app/docs/display-options | Column sets should be personal by default, with an admin "set as tenant default". This fits AWJ's policy rule of configurable but safe defaults. |
| 8 | Group counts and sticky group headers | Each group shows a count, or a total estimate you can toggle. Grouping headers "remain sticky" while scrolling. | https://linear.app/docs/display-options | When grouping by customer or status, show the count or the group total in SAR in the header, toggleable and sticky. |
| 9 | Saved and shared custom views | Save any filtered list as a view with `Option/Alt V`. Views can be personal or shared at workspace or team scope, and can be duplicated. | https://linear.app/docs/custom-views | Give AWJ saved list views, such as "Overdue > 90 days, Dammam branch", with tenant or branch scope that respects branch isolation. |
| 10 | Peek preview | Press `Space` to open peek. Hold `Space` to peek temporarily, and press it again to turn peek off. | https://linear.app/docs/custom-views | Show a quick read-only preview of an invoice or partner from a list, without losing list position. |
| 11 | Two search scopes | `/` searches the whole workspace. `Cmd/Ctrl F` searches within the current view by ID or title. | https://linear.app/docs/search | Offer two searches: global, and find-in-current-list. AWJ should avoid overriding browser `Ctrl F` unless the user opts in. |
| 12 | Sequenced "go to" shortcuts | `G` then `I` opens the Inbox, `G` then `T` opens Triage, and `O` then `V` opens views. `Shift V` opens display options and `Cmd/Ctrl B` switches between list and board. | https://linear.app/docs/inbox ; https://linear.app/docs/triage ; https://linear.app/docs/display-options | Use two-key "go to module" chords, such as G then I for invoices. They work regardless of keyboard layout only if bound to physical key codes. |
| 13 | Triage queue with one-key decisions | Triage lists incoming items for review. Press `1` to accept, `2` to decline, `3` to mark as duplicate and `H` to snooze. Declining sets Canceled and lets you add a comment. | https://linear.app/docs/triage | This is a model for AWJ approval queues, such as late tank readings, store order acceptance or expense approvals: fast keyboard decisions, with a reason when rejecting. |
| 14 | Inbox with priority tab and snooze | The Priority tab separates notifications that need attention. Shortcuts: `U` read/unread, `H` snooze, `Backspace` delete. Snoozed items return later. | https://linear.app/docs/inbox | The AWJ notification centre needs a priority split, such as items blocking period close or ZATCA failures, and snooze. |
| 15 | Undo for reversible bulk ops | Moving issues can be undone with `Cmd/Ctrl Z`, though some fields may not be restored. | https://linear.app/docs/creating-issues | Offer undo only for non-financial metadata. Posted entries need a reversal, not undo, and AWJ should state that difference in the UI. |
| 16 | Principles: purpose-built; simple first, then powerful; no invented terms | "Flexible software lets everyone invent their own workflows, which eventually creates chaos". "Don't invent terms if possible". Start simple and grow more powerful as teams scale. | https://linear.app/method/introduction | Use accounting-standard Arabic terms. Use progressive disclosure so defaults stay simple and power appears through shortcuts and advanced filters. |

## 3. Shopify Polaris (App Design Guidelines on shopify.dev)

Polaris now lives as web components plus App Design Guidelines on shopify.dev. The resource-index template and the index-table composition define the commerce-admin list: search, filters, sort, checkbox bulk selection, row actions revealed on hover, and pagination. The guidelines are explicit about badges, colour roles, density, mobile list transformation, content voice and number formats.

| # | Rule / pattern | Documented detail | Source URL | Implication for AWJ (inference) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Index table anatomy | The index table offers a scannable list with search, filtering, sorting and bulk actions. "Use checkboxes for bulk selection, reveal row actions on hover, and include pagination for large data sets." | https://shopify.dev/docs/api/app-home/latest/patterns/compositions/index-table | Use one canonical AWJ index table for invoices, products and commerce orders. Hover-only row actions also need a keyboard and touch path. |
| 2 | Index page uses wide layout | For resource index pages, set the page `inlineSize` to `large`. This helps with lists "that have many columns". | https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/design/layout | Lists get full width and forms or settings get constrained width. |
| 3 | One severity signal per row | Give each row at most one severity signal, such as one critical badge. Don't repeat it in coloured text or a second badge. | https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/design/layout | Each invoice row gets one status badge. Don't also turn the amount red. Overdue is the badge, and the amount stays neutral. |
| 4 | Row actions: tertiary in last cell; no primary in tables | Row actions go in the last cell as tertiary or icon buttons, or as one menu button. "Avoid primary buttons in tables." | https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/design/layout | Put AWJ row actions in a kebab menu at the inline-end (left in RTL). Never place a "Post" primary button in a row. |
| 5 | Page header: one primary, grouped secondaries, breadcrumbs | The page `primary-action` slot accepts a single primary button. Secondary actions are secondary buttons or a group. Breadcrumbs go in `breadcrumb-actions`. | https://shopify.dev/docs/api/app-home/latest/web-components/layout-and-structure/page | Detail headers should have exactly one primary action, such as Post or Record payment, with the rest in a secondary group or overflow. |
| 6 | Tables become lists on mobile | Tables render "as lists on small screens". Headers carry a `listSlot` of primary or secondary, so data must make sense "as stacked key-value pairs". | https://shopify.dev/docs/api/app-home/latest/web-components/layout-and-structure/table | Define a mobile card mapping per AWJ table (title, status, amount) instead of using horizontal scroll. |
| 7 | Column format types and volume | `format` is `base`, `numeric` or `currency` and controls alignment. Keep column counts minimal. Paginate above 50–100 rows. | https://shopify.dev/docs/api/app-home/latest/web-components/layout-and-structure/table | Declare a column type for amount columns, which drives tabular numerals and end alignment. Paginate on the server. |
| 8 | Empty state: why + one action; empty ≠ filtered | "Say why it's empty, then what to do". Offer one primary action and "Distinguish empty from filtered". Graphics are optional and skipped in dense contexts. | https://shopify.dev/docs/api/app-home/latest/web-components/feedback-and-status-indicators/empty-state | This matches the Stripe rule. AWJ should standardise on a single empty-state component with two variants. |
| 9 | Badge content rules | Use 1–2 words. "Always use past tense for status labels". Use consistent tones per status. Put badges in their own table column. Badges are not for user-created tags. | https://shopify.dev/docs/api/app-home/latest/web-components/feedback-and-status-indicators/badge | Keep the Arabic status vocabulary short, such as مرحَّلة and مدفوعة. Keep one tone map, and keep it separate from tags and labels. |
| 10 | Colour roles = state, never category | Colour roles show state and should never tell categories apart. Green means positive, yellow means paused or incomplete, and orange means pending and is the strongest non-blocking role. Text contrast must be at least 4.5:1. Don't rely on colour alone. | https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/design/visual-design | Status tone must not encode document type or branch. Every coloured status also needs text or an icon. |
| 11 | Banner vs toast vs inline | Banners are page or section alerts that persist until dismissed. Toasts are short and temporary. Inline errors cover field-level issues. | https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/design/user-experience/alerts | This matches Stripe. Locked-period and gate messages are banners, success is a toast, and validation errors are inline. |
| 12 | App window for focused full-screen tasks | Use the app window for complex editors and previews. Prompt to save unsaved changes before exiting. Open it only from a deliberate button. | https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/design/app-structure | The invoice editor with live print preview, and the Store Customizer, should open as focused full-screen views with an unsaved-changes guard. |
| 13 | Plain, consistent language; verb-first CTAs | Use plain language, aiming for grade 7 reading level, with no jargon or idioms. "Use a single noun, verb, or phrase" per concept. CTAs start with a strong verb. | https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/design/content | Keep one Arabic term per concept, such as ترحيل for posting everywhere and never synonyms. A glossary should be enforced in i18n review. |
| 14 | Numbers, dates, currency formatting | Use numerals, with commas for 4+ digits, and don't abbreviate ("12,000", not "12 k"). Write dates with month names, not numerals only. Ranges use an en dash. The currency follows the amount ("$10,000 USD"). | https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/design/content/grammar-and-mechanics | Never abbreviate financial amounts. Write unambiguous dates with month names, such as ١١ ديسمبر ٢٠٢٦, or follow AWJ's 0–9 policy. The currency code goes after the amount, which suits "SAR" and "ر.س" placement. |
| 15 | Mobile-friendly is a quality bar; no page-level horizontal scroll | Built for Shopify requires a responsive app. A rejection reason is when "an entire page requires horizontal scrolling". CLS must be ≤0.1 and LCP ≤2.5 s. | https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/launch/built-for-shopify/requirements | Adopt these as AWJ web quality gates, especially for the commerce workspace and fuel-ops mobile screens. |

## Do not copy

- **Brand identity:** Don't copy Stripe's purple and indigo gradient, Linear's dark-first aesthetic and wordmark style, or Shopify's green and admin chrome. AWJ keeps its single accent and two type families from `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`.
- **Single-letter Latin shortcuts** (Linear's `X`, `J/K`, `F`, `C`; Stripe's `?`): these break or collide on Arabic keyboard layouts. Bind to physical key codes (`event.code`), and always provide a ⌘K and menu equivalent.
- **"Undo" for posted operations** (Linear's `Cmd Z`): this conflicts with AWJ's immutable-ledger rule. Posted entries are reversed by a reversing entry, and the UI must not imply undo.
- **"Mark as paid" status flips** (Stripe's `paid_out_of_band`): in AWJ, "paid" must derive from a posted receipt allocation, not from a status toggle.
- **Stripe invoices editable or deletable in draft without numbering**: this is fine to copy. However, Stripe's "paid → open by unapplying payments" conflicts with AWJ's journal immutability unless it is done through a reversal.
- **Polaris US conventions:** the 12-hour clock with am/pm, US month-first dates, imperial units and hyphenated phone numbers. AWJ follows Saudi and Arabic locale rules, E.164 phone numbers and AWJ's 0–9 digit policy.
- **Polaris badge icon "always appears to the left of the text and can't be repositioned"**: this is LTR-specific. AWJ badges must mirror in RTL.
- **Stripe's "Uncollectible" status**: write-off is an accounting policy decision with ledger effects, and AWJ must not add it as a UI-only status.
- **Hover-only row actions** (Polaris): they fail on touch and keyboard, so AWJ needs a persistent kebab menu or row focus.
